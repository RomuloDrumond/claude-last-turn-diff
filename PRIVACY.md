# Privacy

last-turn-diff collects no personal data and sends nothing off your machine. It runs inside Claude Code: to build the diffs it reads the files Claude edits, just before and after each Edit, Write or NotebookEdit call, and the session's transcript. The diffs stay in the session's memory and end with the session. Two display choices (unified or split view, and line wrap) are saved in Claude Code's plugin store on your machine. It makes no network requests and has no analytics.
